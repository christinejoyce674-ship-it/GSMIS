CSV Format Instructions

Your CSV file must have a header row and follow this column order:

  • learner_id - The unique ID of the learner (e.g., L001)
  • subject_name - Must match the exact subject name in the system
  • term - Numeric (1, 2, or 3)
  • mid - Mid-term mark, numeric (0-100)
  • eot - End-of-term mark, numeric (0-100)

Example: learner_id,subject_name,term,mid,eot
